# Accord Quick Reference

One page per role. Everything starts in the **Collections Console** app (App Launcher → Collections Console). Accounts open as tabs; statements, payments, promises and cases open as subtabs underneath.

---

## Collections rep

**Start your day:** Console home → **Reports → Collections → Collections Work List**. Highest priority first. Accounts on Do Not Contact or holding an active promise aren't on it — that's deliberate.

| To do this | Do this | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Log a call or message | Account → **Log Contact Attempt** → pick an outcome | You can't save without an outcome |
| Record a promise | Log Contact Attempt → **Promise Made**, or New Promise to Pay on the account | Inside policy: account goes on hold until grace ends. Outside policy: Pending Approval, no hold until a supervisor approves. One active promise per account |
| Customer asks you to stop contacting them | Log Contact Attempt → **Requested No Further Contact** | Sets Do Not Contact. The account leaves the work list and automation stops emailing |
| Customer mentions hardship or bankruptcy | Log Contact Attempt → **Hardship or Bankruptcy Mentioned** | Sets Hardship Review. Stop collections activity and tell your supervisor |
| Check a promise's outcome | Account → Promises to Pay | Kept / Broken is set automatically overnight from actual payments. Don't change it by hand |

**You'll never need to** change an account's status. It's set every night from days past due.

## Dispute specialist

| To do this | Do this | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Open a dispute | New Case → **Billing Dispute** → reason + **disputed statement** | No statement, no save. The evidence checklist for the reason appears on the case |
| Know what's due | Case → Milestones | Acknowledge 1 business day · evidence 3 · resolve 10. Status changes complete milestones for you |
| Get the sales rep's side | Case Team → add the rep as **Sales Input** | They can read the case. Access ends automatically when the case closes |
| Credit up to $100 | New Adjustment on the case (Billing Correction, Goodwill Credit or Production Guarantee) | Posts immediately |
| Credit over $100 | New Adjustment → **Submit for Approval** | Case moves to Pending Credit Approval. You can't approve it yourself or mark it Approved by hand |
| Resolve | Status → **Resolved** + **Root Cause** | Root cause required. Withdrawn or duplicate? Use **Closed** instead, no root cause needed |

## Collections supervisor

**Your home page** is the **Collections Supervisor** dashboard and the Default Reviews queue.

| Tile or chart | What a bad day looks like |
|---|---|
| Accounts to work today | Rising faster than the team can call |
| Dispute milestones, last 30 days | Any red slice. Open **Open Milestones Closest to Breach** to see which case is next |
| Default reviews opened on time | Anything not on time. Every account at 90+ days needs a review decision |
| Promise outcomes | Broken outpacing Kept means promises are being accepted that customers can't keep |
| Resolved disputes by root cause | One root cause dominating. "Concession Not Recorded at Signing" is a sales process problem, not a collections one |

**Approvals:** credits over $100 and out-of-policy promises come to you. Rejecting a credit? Enter the rejection reason — if it's missed, you'll get a task to add it.

**Alerts:** you get an email when a dispute milestone is breached. The case owner gets a warning at 75% of the time.

## Sales rep

| To do this | Do this | Watch for |
|---|---|---|
| Work new inquiries | Leads → Inbound Leads queue | A "possible duplicate" alert means this person is already a customer. Check before converting |
| Close a deal with a concession | Add a **Commitment** before Closed Won | Concession Offered = Yes with no Commitment blocks Closed Won |
| Asked about a dispute | You'll be added to the case team | Read the case and respond. Access ends when it closes |

---

**Something not working?** Note the record, what you clicked, and the exact message. Messages in Accord tell you what to fix — if one doesn't, that's a bug worth reporting.
